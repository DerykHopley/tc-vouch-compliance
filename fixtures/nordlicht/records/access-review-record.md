# Account and access rights review, first half of 2026

## Scope

All accounts in the directory, the ERP and the CAD system: 171 employee accounts, 9 administrator accounts, 14 supplier accounts and 6 technical accounts. Accounts that our staff hold in customers' systems were not part of this review.

## Result

| Area | Reviewed by | Date | Accounts | Accounts removed | Rights removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employee accounts | Team managers | 2026-06-20 | 171 | 0 | 11 |
| Roles and their rights | Role owners | 2026-06-27 | 14 roles | n/a | 6 |
| Administrator accounts | IT operations lead | 2026-06-27 | 9 | 0 | 1 |
| Supplier accounts | Head of purchasing | 2026-07-03 | 14 | 3 | n/a |
| Technical accounts | IT operations lead | 2026-07-03 | 6 | 0 | 2 |

## Follow-up

Every employee account belonged to a current employee. The 11 employee rights removed were left over from earlier role changes; the administrator right removed was a second server group no longer needed.

The three supplier accounts removed belonged to jobs that had already finished, and had not been disabled when they ended. Purchasing now tells IT the day a supplier's job ends.

All removals were done by 10 July 2026. The previous review was completed in January 2026.
