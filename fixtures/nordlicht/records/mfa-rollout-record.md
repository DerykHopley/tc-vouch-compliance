# Sign-in settings and second-factor rollout

## Directory password settings

Screenshot notes from the directory's password settings, 14 August 2026: minimum length 12 characters; breached-password check on; no expiry timer; account locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed attempts.

## Second factor

| System | Reachable from | Second factor | Enforced since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | Internet | Authenticator app | 2025-11-03 |
| Supplier portal | Internet | Authenticator app | 2026-01-19 |
| ERP | Office network | None | n/a |

All 171 employee accounts have an authenticator app registered. Employees sign in to the outside services in the cloud services register with their company account, so the same password settings and second factor apply.

## Administrator accounts

All 9 administrator accounts are separate from their owners' everyday accounts and require a hardware security key since 15 April 2026.
