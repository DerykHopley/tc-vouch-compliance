# Approved cloud services register

## Approved services

These services are run for us by outside providers. IT and the information security officer approve each one before it is used, after checking the contract, where the data is kept, and the provider's latest independent audit report or security certificate.

| Service | Used for | Owner | Highest classification | Data location | Contract | Provider report or certificate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-learning platform | Security and compliance courses and their completion records | HR manager | Internal | Data centres in Germany | Service contract and data processing agreement, signed 2025-11-20 | Provider's security certificate, valid to 2027-10-31; checked 2025-11-12 |
| Mail filter | Spam and malware filtering of incoming and outgoing mail | IT operations lead | Internal; confidential mail passes through it only end-to-end encrypted | Data centres in Germany and the Netherlands | Service contract and data processing agreement, renewed 2026-06-01 | Independent audit report on the provider's controls for 2025; checked 2026-05-18 |
| Payroll service | Monthly payroll and payslips | HR manager | Confidential | Data centre in Germany | Service contract and data processing agreement, signed 2023-01-10 | Provider's security certificate, valid to 2028-03-31; checked 2026-03-02 |

## Monitoring the providers offer

Each provider offers ways to watch its service: the e-learning platform and the payroll service keep a sign-in log that administrators can export, and the mail filter sends alert emails and shows a dashboard of blocked messages. None of this was looked at when the services were approved, and nobody at Nordlicht has been given the job of watching it.

## Yearly check

On 22 September 2026 the IT operations lead confirmed that the three services above are the only outside services in use, and that each contract and provider certificate or report is current.
