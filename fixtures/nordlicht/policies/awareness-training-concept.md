# Awareness and training concept

## Topics

The yearly security training covers: our information security policy, how to report a security event, what to do when malware is suspected, password and login rules, keeping customer information confidential under a non-disclosure agreement, and the rule that company information stays on company systems, never in private online storage.

## Who gets what

All employees take the basic online course. Administrators take an extra session on privileged accounts. Managers take a session on their part in access approvals and incident handling. Staff who work on customer networks or in prototype areas receive the customer-specific briefing before they start.

## When

The basic course runs every year in the first quarter. New employees take it within their first month. After a serious incident or a new kind of phishing attack, the information security officer sends a short briefing to everyone affected.

## Records

Our training server records who completed each course and when. HR follows up with anyone who has not finished within four weeks.

## Contact

The information security officer and the security desk are named in every course and on the intranet as the people to ask about security.
