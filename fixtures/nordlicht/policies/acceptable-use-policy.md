# Acceptable use policy

## Company devices

Company laptops and phones are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed when it does not affect work.

## Your sign-in details

Your password is yours alone. Do not tell it to anyone, not even your manager or the IT service desk. Do not write it down or keep it in a plain file; use the company password manager. Do not use your company password for any private account. If you think someone may know your password, change it at once and tell the security desk.

## Lost devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the security hotline straight away.
