# Account management procedure

## One person, one account

Every person who works with our systems gets their own named account. Accounts are never shared between people.

## Shared accounts

A shared account is allowed only for a display terminal in the production hall that cannot change any data. Each one is listed in the account register with the reason it exists and the manager responsible for it.

## New accounts

A manager requests an account through a ticket in the IT service portal. IT creates it only after a second person, the system owner, has approved the request. New accounts start with the standard employee role, which can use email, the intranet and office software and nothing else; further rights are requested separately under the access control policy.

## Changes and leavers

HR raises a ticket when someone changes job or leaves. IT disables a leaver's account by the end of their last working day, and deletes it after 90 days.

## Service providers

An external service provider's account is set up with an end date. IT disables it when the job is done, or on the end date, whichever comes first.

## Handing over sign-in details

IT never sends a starting password by email. The new user receives it in person, or by text message to their own phone, and must change it at first sign-in.

## Account reviews

Every six months, each manager checks the list of accounts in their team, and IT removes any account that is no longer needed. The review is recorded.
