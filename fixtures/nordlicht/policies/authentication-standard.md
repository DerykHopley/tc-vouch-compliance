# Authentication standard

## How we chose our sign-in methods

We decide how people sign in to a system by looking at what could go wrong with it. Systems reachable from the internet, such as email, the supplier portal and our approved cloud services, face password guessing and phishing, so they need a second factor. Systems reachable only from the office network may rely on a password alone. The IT operations lead makes this choice when a system is introduced, and looks at it again when the system's exposure changes.

## Passwords

Passwords have at least 12 characters. The directory blocks passwords found in known breach lists. Passwords do not expire on a timer, but must be changed at once if they may have been exposed.

## Second factor

All internet-facing systems require an authenticator app as a second factor. We follow the national cyber security authority's current guidance on sign-in methods.

## Administrator accounts

Administrators use a separate administrator account for admin work, never their everyday account. Administrator accounts always require a hardware security key.
