# Information security policy

## Purpose

Nordlicht Komponenten GmbH builds sensor housings and wiring harnesses for car makers. Our customers trust us with drawings and prototype data, so protecting information is part of delivering a good product. This policy sets out why information security matters to us and what we aim for.

## Objectives

We keep customer and company information confidential, correct and available when it is needed. In particular we aim to pass our customers' TISAX assessment, to let no customer data leave the company without authorisation, and to bring any business system back within one working day after a disruption.

## Where our security requirements come from

The information security officer works out our security requirements each year from three inputs: the business plan agreed by management, the security clauses in our customer contracts, and the laws that apply to us in Germany, including data protection law. The requirements are written down in the security requirements list, which is kept with this policy and updated when the business plan or a major contract changes.

## Approval

Management approved this policy on 14 July 2026. It is signed by both managing directors.

## Supporting standards

This policy is backed by topic standards and procedures, each with its own owner: the access control policy, the acceptable use policy, the authentication standard, the account management procedure, the encryption standard, the network and transfer standard, the logging standard, the security event reporting procedure and the awareness and training concept.

## Publication

This policy and all supporting standards are published on the intranet under "Security". New employees receive a link to them on their first day.

## Changes

When this policy or a supporting standard changes in a way that affects people, the information security officer sends a short change notice to all employees. Suppliers and service providers who work with our information receive the notice through the supplier portal.

## Review

The information security officer reviews this policy every year and after any serious security incident, and proposes changes to management. The supporting standards and procedures are reviewed at least every two years by their owners, and also after any serious security incident or major change.
