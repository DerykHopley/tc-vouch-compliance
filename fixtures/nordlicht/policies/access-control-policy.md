# Access control policy

## Our approach

Access to information is granted on the basis of the job a person does. We start from no access and add only what the job needs.

## Roles

Rights are bundled into roles, such as purchasing, engineering, production planning and finance. Each role has an owner who decides what it contains.

## Asking for access

A manager asks for a role or an extra right through a ticket. The owner of the system approves it before IT grants it. Rights that are not needed for the job are refused.

## Changes of job

When someone moves to a new job, IT removes the rights of the old role at the same time as it grants the new one.

## Removing access

Rights that are no longer needed are removed within five working days of the manager telling IT.

## Privileged rights

Ordinary accounts never hold administrator rights. Administrator rights are given only to separate administrator accounts.

## Reviews

The access rights of all accounts, including administrator and technical accounts, are reviewed every six months by the role owners.
