# Encryption policy

Owner: Information security officer. Version 2.1, approved March 2026.

## Laptops

Every company laptop uses full-disk encryption. IT turns it on before a laptop is handed out, and users cannot turn it off.

## Recovery keys

Recovery keys are stored in the central device management system. Users do not keep a copy of their own recovery key.

## Monthly check

IT checks the encryption status of every laptop each month, using the device management report. A laptop found unencrypted is locked until it is fixed.
