# Access rights policy

## Principle

Nobody gets access to information because it might be useful one day. Each person gets the access their current tasks need, and no more.

## Roles

Access rights are grouped into roles that match our functions, for example sales, design, production and accounting. For each role, a role owner in the business decides which rights it holds.

## Requesting and approving access

Access is requested by the person's line manager in the IT ticket system, naming the role or the single right needed. The owner of the system checks the request against the person's tasks and approves or refuses it; the information security officer approves every request for access to confidential information. IT grants nothing without both approvals.

## Privileged rights

Administrator rights are given only to separate administrator accounts, never to a person's everyday account.

## Changing tasks

When someone takes on other tasks, their manager asks for the new role, and IT removes the rights of the old role on the same day the new ones are granted.

## Removing rights

Rights that are no longer needed are removed. When someone leaves, all their rights end with their accounts under the user account standard.

## Periodic access review

Every six months, each system owner reviews who holds which rights in their system: employee accounts, administrator accounts and technical accounts. Our staff's accounts in customers' systems are reviewed by the manager responsible for that customer. Rights that are no longer needed are removed, and the review is recorded with its date and result.
