# Awareness and training concept

## Aim

Everyone who works for us should know the risks of handling our information and what they can do about them.

## Topics

The yearly security course covers:

- the information security policy and where to find it,
- what to do when a computer shows signs of malware,
- passwords, tokens and the user account standard,
- the rules that customers' contracts and the law set for us,
- non-disclosure agreements before sharing protected information,
- using cloud services and other IT services from outside.

## Target groups

Some groups get an extra module on their own risks: managers, administrators, staff who work in customers' networks, and the production staff on the shop floor.

## When training takes place

New staff take the course in their first month. Everyone repeats it each year. After a serious incident, or when a new kind of attack appears, the information security officer sends a short awareness note or holds an extra session.

## Records

HR keeps a record of who took which course, and when.
