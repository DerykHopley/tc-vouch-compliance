# Weekly log checks, third quarter of 2026

## Checks done

All 13 weekly checks of the quarter were done, by one of the two administrators, each within its week. Each administrator's own actions in the logs are checked by the other one. The checks covered the alerts from the central log server, the firewall and the intrusion detection system, and the VPN's start and end times of each remote session.

| Week | Checked by | Alerts | Findings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27 to 30 | Administrator A | 41 | Failed sign-ins from one laptop with an old saved password; password updated |
| 31 to 34 | Administrator B | 37 | None beyond expected alerts |
| 35 to 39 | Administrator A | 58 | A supplier's VPN session left open overnight; closed, and the job's account disabled |

## Follow-up

The open VPN session was passed to the incident team, which classed it as a low-priority vulnerability.
