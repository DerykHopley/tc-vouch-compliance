Vouch · TISAX audit-readiness agent · eval report

Which model should assess the evidence?

Each model assessed the same 12 VDA ISA controls for a fictional company through Vouch's agent, and each verdict was compared with an expected answer. The runs were on 5 November 2026. Every number here is built from the raw results committed in evals/, without calling a model.

What the runs show

  • Sonnet 5.5 is the most accurate through Vouch's agent: on Nordlicht it gets 76% of the requirement verdicts and 75% of the control verdicts right, at about $0.10 per assessment.
  • GPT-6 Luna is the best value. It scores 68%, against GPT-6.1 Sol's 69%, at about one eighteenth of Sol's cost ($0.003 against $0.057 per assessment), and none of its cases failed.
  • A router came close to Sonnet. Jev Router, which picks a model for each call, gets 70% of verdicts and the same 75% of controls as Sonnet, at a third of Sonnet's cost.
  • Most of the lost points come from Vouch's search loop, not from the models' judgement. With all 20 evidence documents in the prompt, Sonnet reaches 90% instead of 76%, and Haiku 80% instead of 51%. That is follow-up #188: assess a small profile with all of its evidence in one prompt.
  • The local model is not usable yet. gpt-oss 20b, run on a home GPU, failed 7 of 12 cases: it ran out of its call budget or wrote tool calls Vouch couldn't read.
  • Read Alpenwerk's scores with care. Its expected answers were drafted by an agent, and the owner has checked only 5 of its 100. Nordlicht's were all reviewed.

How it was measured

The task
Vouch assesses one VDA ISA 2027.1 control at a time. It searches the company's evidence, quotes it, and gives each of the control's requirements a verdict: evidenced, partly evidenced or not evidenced. The profiles are two fictional companies: Nordlicht (84 requirements, 20 documents) and Alpenwerk (100 requirements).
Verdicts right
Requirements whose verdict matches the expected answer.
Controls right
Controls whose rolled-up verdict matches the expected one. The control is evidenced only when every counted requirement is evidenced.
Citations expected
Of the documents cited after Vouch's quote check, the share that the expected answer also cites.
Faithfulness
DeepEval's score of how closely each answer sticks to the passages it cites, as a mean over the answers the judge could score. GPT-6.1 Terra judged the Anthropic, local and router runs. Sonnet 5.5 judged the OpenAI runs, so no OpenAI model judged OpenAI's answers.
Jev Router
TypeSafe's router on OpenRouter: each call may go to a different model, at that model's price. These results record the router but not which models it picked; recording that is #185.
Expected answers
Nordlicht's were reviewed by the owner (#111). Alpenwerk's were drafted by an agent, and the owner has checked 5 of 100 (#50). A model that judges the way the drafter did scores higher there.
Limits
Each model ran once on each profile, so one or two verdicts can move a score by a few points. Mistral was left out: Small is blocked by an OpenRouter account setting, and Large's estimate was over the day's budget.
