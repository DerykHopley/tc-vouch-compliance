# Customer System Access Review, second quarter of 2026

## Scope

The accounts that Nordlicht staff hold in our customers' systems: the car makers' supplier portals, their engineering data exchanges and the test benches on customer networks. The customers issue these accounts, so they are not in our directory and are outside the half-yearly account and access rights review. This is the first review of them.

## Result

| Customer system | Reviewed by | Date | Accounts | Accounts removed | Rights reduced |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer A supplier portal | Key account manager, customer A | 2026-06-24 | 9 | 2 | 1 |
| Customer A engineering data exchange | Head of engineering | 2026-06-24 | 6 | 1 | 0 |
| Customer B supplier portal | Key account manager, customer B | 2026-06-30 | 7 | 1 | 0 |
| Customer B test bench network | Head of engineering | 2026-07-02 | 4 | 0 | 1 |
| Customer C supplier portal | Key account manager, customer C | 2026-07-02 | 5 | 1 | 0 |

## Follow-up

Each account was checked against the list of current employees and their jobs. Two accounts belonged to employees who had left the company in the spring: their access to our own systems ended on their last day, but the customers' accounts had stayed open. Three more belonged to staff who had moved to jobs with no customer work. The customers closed all five at our request by 7 July 2026 and confirmed it in writing.

Two accounts held more than the job needed (upload rights where viewing was enough) and were reduced to viewing.

From now on, IT tells the key account manager on a person's last day or change of job, and the key account manager asks the customer to close or change the account within five working days. The review is repeated every quarter.
