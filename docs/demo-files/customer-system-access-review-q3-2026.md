# Customer System Access Review, third quarter of 2026

## Scope

The accounts that Nordlicht staff hold in our customers' systems: the car makers' supplier portals, their engineering data exchanges and the test benches on customer networks. This is the second quarterly review; the first, for the second quarter, was completed on 7 July 2026.

## Result

| Customer system | Reviewed by | Date | Accounts | Accounts removed | Rights reduced |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer A supplier portal | Key account manager, customer A | 2026-09-23 | 7 | 0 | 0 |
| Customer A engineering data exchange | Head of engineering | 2026-09-23 | 5 | 1 | 0 |
| Customer B supplier portal | Key account manager, customer B | 2026-09-29 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| Customer B test bench network | Head of engineering | 2026-10-01 | 4 | 0 | 0 |
| Customer C supplier portal | Key account manager, customer C | 2026-10-01 | 4 | 0 | 1 |
| Customer D supplier portal (new this quarter) | Key account manager, customer D | 2026-10-02 | 3 | 0 | 0 |

## Follow-up

Each account was checked against the list of current employees and their jobs. One engineering data exchange account belonged to an engineer who left on 31 August 2026 and had not been closed: the customer closed it on 25 September 2026 at our request.

One portal account at customer C held an approval right its holder no longer needs after a change of job; the customer withdrew it on 6 October 2026.

Customer D, won in August, issued three portal accounts this quarter. Each was asked for through a ticket and approved by the head of sales before the customer created it.

The five-working-day notice to key account managers, started after the last review, reached the customer in time for every other leaver and job change this quarter. The next review is due in January 2027.
