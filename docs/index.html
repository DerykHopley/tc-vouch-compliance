ISMS and TISAX audit-readiness agent

Vouch

Vouch helps a compliance lead prepare for an information security audit (TISAX, ISO 27001) by tracing each control back to the company's own evidence.

An assessment searches the company's policies and records for one control, gives each requirement a verdict (evidenced, partly evidenced or not evidenced) backed by citations quoted word for word, and drafts an answer for the auditor. The compliance lead reviews every verdict in three steps, Confirm findings, Plan fixes and Accept, and only accepted verdicts count towards readiness.
